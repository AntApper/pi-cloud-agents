## Security & Architecture Verification Checklist
- Keepalive: Controller Lambda runs every minute, mints 5-minute scoped proxy tokens for port 8080 (`CreateMicrovmAuthToken`), and pings `GET /v1/status` to maintain inbound proxy traffic activity.
- Idle policy: When `suggestedAction === "suspend"` and `idleSince` exceeds `idleGraceSec`, triggers `SuspendMicrovm` from outside (as MicroVM cannot suspend itself).
- Rate limit handling: Suspends with backoff and jitter to comply with the 2 TPS `SuspendMicrovm` service quota.
- Max duration & terminal state cleanup: Enforces the 8-hour hard lifetime (`maxDurationSec + 10 min`) and terminates finished/failed runs exceeding a 10-minute grace window.
- Unhealthy VM detection: Tracks consecutive failed pings in `controller/health/<microvmId>.json` and terminates VMs unreachable for 3+ polls and > 15 minutes.
- Janitor: Automatically force-deletes `runs/<runId>/*` secrets in AWS Secrets Manager upon observing `TERMINATED` MicroVMs.
- Execution summary: Produces `controller/last-run.json` in S3 with structured metrics and decision tracking.
- Zero secrets in logs or manifests; dry-run support (`DRY_RUN=true`).
- Unblocks Gate G3 (Live infra smoke).
